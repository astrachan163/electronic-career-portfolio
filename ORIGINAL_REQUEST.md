- **Resume**
- **Career Summary:** career choice, description of the career, skills and education required, and future job outlook (pay, advancement)
- **Sample Materials:**
  - *Career-Related Education:* school activities, career research projects, application of business education, and related occupational skills, plus how each relates to the career
  - *Educational Enhancement:* career development planning, job shadowing, work-based learning, internships, apprenticeships, informational interviews, community service, and products developed
  - *Special Skills:* up to five skills, talents or abilities related to career goals. Audio and video may be included.
