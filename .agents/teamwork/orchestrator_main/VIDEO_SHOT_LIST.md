## Recording Guidelines for All Shots
- **Resolution**: 1920×1080 (1080p) or 1280×720 (720p) in 16:9 widescreen.
- **Recording tool**: QuickTime Player (`Cmd + Shift + 5` → Record Selected Portion or Entire Screen).
- **Target duration**: 10 to 15 seconds per clip (focused, crisp actions).
- **Audio**: No audio needed (clips will play silently with ambient or interactive controls).
- **Format**: MP4 (H.264) or WebM.
- **Current status in project**: Polished placeholder posters and high-resolution screenshot stills from previous test runs are already wired in the site and will seamlessly update whenever new video files are dropped into the target paths below.
